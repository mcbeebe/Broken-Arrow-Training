The one-paragraph takeaway

The market splits into four camps — the analytics incumbent (TrainingPeaks: human coaching at $149–359/mo), the consumer champion (Runna: best-in-class onboarding & watch handoff, but pace-targets-only adaptivity, no biometrics, ultras capped at 50K), the free device-native substitute (Garmin: readiness-driven daily workouts, periodized strength, PacePro — free with the watch), and the AI-native cohort (Athletica, Vert.run, TriDot, Humango: $15–29/mo, season grammar, advisory AI). Attune's verified differentiators are real and rare: methodology depth, trail/ultra physics wired into prescription, a readiness engine biometrics actually drive, an LLM coach that proposes one-tap plan edits with honest advisories, and midlife/masters depth nobody else has. The two verified open lanes: ① multi-event season periodization — premium-monetized across the market, missing in Runna/Garmin, and no app periodizes a running race + a Hyrox in one season (Runna, the official Hyrox partner, resolves it with advice, not product — Attune already owns both engines); ② the execution loop — watch-first delivery is where consumer trust is won, and web-first delivery is Attune's largest structural exposure (its Garmin workout push already exists, underleveraged).

Structural fact #1 — personalization pricing

Adaptive personalization at TrainingPeaks is human ($149–359/mo) — 10–30× the algorithmic products. Multi-event support is consistently a paid differentiator (TP ATP Premium-gated; Humango gates multi-goal to $28.99/mo).

Structural fact #2 — the free substitute

Garmin anchors adaptive coaching at $0 for watch owners. A paid app wins only on what Garmin doesn't do: run/ultra fueling (none on the platform), injury accommodation (no input exists), multi-race planning (one primary race; documented post-race "stuck in recovery" failures), methodology choice, human-legible rationale. Line for line, Attune's strengths.

The Strava–Runna axis

Acquisition closed May 22, 2025; apps kept separate "for the foreseeable future" (still true 14 months on). The $149.99/yr bundle (annual-only, sold exclusively through Strava) anchors "social + AI training plans." Strava — 100M+ users — bought coaching rather than building it.

04

Where Attune wins today (verified)

1 · Readiness that acts

Only Garmin and Attune wire biometrics into what the athlete is told to do — and Garmin can't take an injury input, explain itself, or converse. Athletica "advises, not acts"; Runna has none (verified).

2 · Descent/DOMS science

Eccentric dose → kinetic curve → repeated-bout protection → load dampening. No competitor analog (Vert's Mountain Index is a progress tracker, not a recovery model).

3 · Integrated trail craft

Per-race fueling (g/hr + gut training), heat/altitude blocks, vert + downhill prescription, power-hiking, dress rehearsal — vs. Vert's standalone calculators, Runna's 50K cap, Garmin's total fueling absence.

4 · The honest LLM coach

Market-validated by counterexample: Strava/Garmin AI text is mocked for restating numbers, fixed tone, no training narrative (2026 r/Strava study). Attune's grounded-context + persona + integrity architecture is the published antidote pattern.

5 · Methodology pluralism

Nine cited methods with fit-based selection vs. one house algorithm everywhere else.

6 · Midlife/masters depth

Menopause status + symptoms + bone-loading finishers + masters recovery doctrine + evidence-humble cycle awareness. Runna's post-natal plans are the only comparable gesture in the set.

Ranked gaps & opportunities — Witchel check inline

G1 · Multi-event season engine (A/B/C races, recover→bridge→build chaining)

The market's premium feature (TP ATP, Humango's $28.99 gate), absent in Runna/Garmin — and nobody chains running + Hyrox. Attune already owns both engines, post-race recovery formulas, phase-aware strength, and a real TSB engine (Friel's tier targets directly implementable). Design in §7.

✅ Massive — every racer books multiple events/yr; hybrid is the fastest-growing segment✅ Visceral — the post-race "now what?" void; Garmin's stuck-in-recovery threads show it live✅ Customer words — "plan my season," "my A race"

G2 · Close the execution loop (watch-first delivery)

Runna's moat; the industry's trust surface. Attune already pushes structured workouts to Garmin — underleveraged: surface it, extend windows, auto-resync on edit. Then Apple Watch push (WorkoutKit) + a glanceable today surface.

✅ Massive — execution happens on-wrist✅ Visceral — nobody memorizes intervals✅ "Send it to my watch"

G3 · Onboarding as belief-building + time-to-first-value

Runna's ~30-screen flow with a value preview is the category benchmark; fitness apps lose ~75%+ of users in 3 days; NN/g: ask only what changes the plan, teach in context. Attune's onboarding is information-rich but form-like; the welcome letter is a strong asset arriving late. Redesign: goal-first, live plan preview mid-flow, defer nice-to-haves into contextual asks.

✅✅✅ — "show me my plan before you ask me to believe"

G4 · Plan realignment as first-class UI

Runna's trust moment (fires only after >3 misses — beatable). Silent drift is one failure mode; silent auto-change is the other (most-criticized AI pattern). Prompt + explain + one-tap is the verified sweet spot — and Attune's plan-edit proposal machinery already exists. Build: missed-workout detector → coach-authored realignment card.

✅✅✅

G5 · Performance-adaptive pace targets

Runna's one true adaptive signal ("Pace Insights"). Attune has richer inputs (compliance grades, GAP, time-in-zone) + a VDOT engine but never recalibrates targets from actuals. Opt-in, targets-only, explained.

✅✅✅ — "my paces got stale"

G6 · Course-aware race pacing — PacePro-for-trail

Garmin computes grade-adjusted split bands (road-strong, free); Vert offers finish scenarios; Runna explicitly declines. Attune has course segments + Minetti GAP + vert physics — a trail pace-band generator with fueling checkpoints would be category-first. Start with the 3 BA courses.

✅ Massive — race day is the point✅ Visceral — blowing up from bad pacing✅ "What pace on the climbs?"

G7 · Intensity-distribution monitor ("gray-zone guard") + durability metric

We have per-session time-in-zone + method targets; nobody in the set computes polarization compliance or aerobic decoupling for athletes. "Your easy days are too hard" is the most-quoted coaching truth in the category — Garmin's DSW praise centers on exactly this.

✅✅✅

G8 · Readiness UX hardening

Orthosomnia-safe red states (pair every red with a concrete action — the deload program exists, surface it), trend-over-number, non-color redundant encoding (~8% of men are red-green colorblind; masters-skewed), plain-language metric names at default detail level (TP renamed CTL/ATL/TSB — we already have detail levels). Cheap polish pass.

✅✅retention more than acquisition

G9 · Flexible-consistency mechanics (not streaks)

Rigid streaks verifiably backfire where rest is programmed (BJHP 2025; Milkman 2021). "Sessions completed vs planned this week — rest counts as compliance" + grace framing. Small feature on the existing compliance engine.

✅✅✅

G10 · Free standalone tools as acquisition funnel

Vert runs free calculators at standalone URLs as top-of-funnel. Attune's fueling/vert/heat research is deeper — publish 2–3 free tools (trail fueling planner, vert-adjusted finish predictor, race-day heat planner).

✅ reach✅ real pre-purchase job✅ customer words

Deliberately not ranked: social feed (Strava owns it), human-coach marketplace (TP owns it), audio-guided runs (NRC gives them away), triathlon depth (TriDot/Athletica's lane).

UX/UI — the five patterns that won (2024–2026)

1 · Verdict-first, data underneath

One large score/state + short narrative + progressive disclosure (WHOOP's 72-pt score, 3 tiers; Garmin Morning Report; TP's Fresh/Optimal/Overloading renames). Attune: has score + narrative; polish per G8.

2 · Proactive morning narrative

WHOOP Daily Outlook, Oura Advisor (user-selectable tone — Attune's persona editor already exceeds this), Garmin Morning Report. Attune: proactive pings exist; package as a true morning-report surface.

3 · Adaptation prompts as first-class UI

Runna's realignment prompt is the trust moment reviewers cite. Attune: G4.

4 · AI text that adds context, never restates stats

The 2026 r/Strava study's four tensions (numerical-vs-contextual, isolated-vs-narrative, fixed tone, one-voice) are design requirements. Garmin Connect+ shows the cost of shipping beneath the bar — and of paywalling before clearing it. Attune: architecture aligned; hold the bar.

5 · Belief-building onboarding with value preview

Runna: goal-first contextual questions, real-race lookup, "with/without" race-time preview, social proof clustered pre-paywall — it beat Garmin at plan creation despite Garmin owning years of the athlete's data. Attune: G3.

Design-science checklist (evidence-based)

  • Ask only what changes the plan; teach contextually (NN/g) · first completed workout inside week one is the retention hinge (~75%+ 3-day DAU loss; day-30 ~5–12%, top ~25%).
  • First-class BCT objects: goals, self-monitoring, feedback, prompts, social support (PMC10545861) · no rigid streaks — weekly consistency with rest-as-compliance (BJHP 2025; Milkman MS 2021).
  • Few, smart notifications (evening preview + morning readiness); generic nudges verifiably don't move behavior.
  • Readiness: traffic-light + expandable factors; orthosomnia-safe reds (concrete action, trend-first) · "why this workout" one-liner w/ optional depth; never silent plan changes.
  • Masters accessibility: 16px+ body floor, generous targets, WCAG 4.5:1, non-color redundancy on every zone/status color · measure sessions-completed-as-prescribed, not DAU.

Multi-event periodization — grammar, science, and the Attune Season Engine

7.1 The converged product grammar (verified across 7 apps)

  • Only A races get a full peak + taper; B races get a race-week mini-taper + enforced post-race recovery; C races are trained through.
  • ≥8 weeks between peaks (TrainerRoad hard rule; TriDot guidance); A-to-A within ~32 weeks (TP).
  • Proximity threshold below which a secondary race is trained through: ~7–10 days (Runna) to ~2 weeks (Athletica, founder-confirmed).
  • The post-race state machine is where engines break in production (Garmin stuck-in-recovery; TrainerRoad VO2-after-race). Explicit inter-event transitions are a requirement.

7.3 Design sketch — the Attune Season Engine (half → Hyrox → marathon)

RACEA/B/C tagged→ RECOVER1 d/mi · reverse taper (shipped R5)→ BRIDGEresidual-aware quality swap→ BUILDcompressed · ≥8 wk to peak→ TAPERTSB tier targets (shipped engine)→ RACEnext event
  • Race calendar w/ A/B/C priorities. A races get full blocks; B races get a mini-taper inside the active block + tune-up placement 4–6 wk out feeding the existing predictor rehearsal; C races get a day-type stamp.
  • Residual-aware bridges — the category-first piece. Half→Hyrox: hold aerobic with 1–2 intensity touches (Hickson '81) while concentrating strength-endurance/glycolytic (shortest residuals → last). Hyrox→marathon: hold strength at 1×/wk (Bickel) while rebuilding run volume (Wilson: acceptable — strength is maintenance-only). Every component engine already exists in Attune.
  • Season-level feasibility honesty — extend the shipped advisory layer: "two A races 5 weeks apart — the second gets a compressed build; consider B-tagging one" (the exact failure TrainerRoad/Athletica users hit).
  • Coach narration — a SEASON context section: where the athlete is in the chain, what quality this block protects, why today serves the next race. No competitor's AI can explain a season.
  • Monetization: the market prices this as premium (Humango $28.99/mo gate; TP Premium).

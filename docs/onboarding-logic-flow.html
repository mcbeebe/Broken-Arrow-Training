How the app turns onboarding answers into a plan

An executive map of every decision, input, and function that converts a new athlete's onboarding answers into a personalized training plan — traced end-to-end for real athletes and verified against the live engine, not hand-waved.

The one fork that decides everything

The first onboarding question — “What are you training for?” — routes the athlete to one of three independent engines. Everything downstream depends on this choice.

Trail / Road Race → method engine Hyrox → template engine General Fitness → 4-pillar engine

Where your three examples land

There is no separate “road” type. A road marathon is Trail / Road Race + marathon. So all three of your examples — the 2:50 marathoner, the 55-year-old, and the 100-mile ultra — run through the same method-based running engine, differing only by distance, goal time, and demographics.

How correctness is confirmed

A harness runs the real engine functions over fixed personas and writes deterministic JSON. This page embeds that output as ground truth, and the live explorer below re-derives the same numbers in your browser — every persona is checked ✓ against the engine in §11.

Each pathway, step by step

🏔 Trail / Road Race
  1. Race distance gate: trail — 5K…100mi/mtn. Sets terrain & eligible methods.inferTerrain() · applicability.byDistance
  2. Method selection (post-onboarding screen) scores all 8 methods, shows top-3.selectMethods() → 3·dist + 2·exp + 1·terrain
  3. Athlete picks one of the three → selectedMethodId.
  4. Generate periodized plan: paces, phases, ramp, taper.generatePlanFromMethod()
  5. Overlays: goal-pace blend (anchor + goal), injury lead-in, menopause “Strength + bone”.
🏋 Hyrox
  1. Weak station gate: hyrox — SkiErg…Wall Balls. Becomes a focus label only.
  2. Experience level alone fixes total weeks (8–20) & the whole block structure.getLevelParams(experienceLevel)
  3. Days/week maps to a fixed role list (run · strength · stations · long).
  4. Generate from hand-authored templates.generateHyroxPlan()
  5. Ignores menopause · sex · injury · equipment · strength/cross · anchor · goal.
🌱 General Fitness
  1. Main goal gate: general — stay-healthy / lose-fat / build-muscle / build-endurance → preset.GOAL_PRESETS[generalGoal]
  2. Cardio modality gate: general — run / bike / row / swim / mixed (labels sessions).
  3. Block length: date → 4–16 wk, else rolling 12-wk. Deload every 4th week.resolveBlockWeeks()
  4. Generate 4-pillar rolling plan: Zone2 · VO₂max · strength · mobility.generateGeneralFitnessPlan()
  5. Overlay: midlife bone finisher + SIT hard day + symptom-keyed recovery nudge.

Pace math — Daniels VDOT

src/engines/planGenerator/vdot.ts. A race time → VDOT, then each zone’s pace is solved back from a % of VDOT.

VO₂
-4.6 + 0.182258·v + 0.000104·v²
%VO₂max
0.8 + 0.1894·e^(-0.0128t) + 0.2990·e^(-0.1933t)
VDOT
VO₂ / %VO₂max

Zone intensities (% of VDOT)

Goal-pace blend. Only when the athlete gives both a current race anchor and a goal time, and the goal is a stretch, quality paces sharpen from current→goal across the build — capped at min(goalVDOT, currentVDOT × 1.08). Volume is not raised to chase the goal.

Method selection — a weighted score, then top-3

score = 3·distance + 2·experience + 1·terrain (methodSelection.ts; BEST 4 · GOOD 3 · OK 2 · CAUTION 1 · NOT_SUITED 0). Methods that are NOT_SUITED for the distance are dropped unless fewer than three remain; ties break alphabetically by id.

Engine C — General Fitness rolling 4-pillar

generateGeneralFitnessPlan(config, today) (src/engines/generalFitness/index.ts:323). A method-less, goal-weighted engine over four pillars — Zone 2, VO₂max, strength, mobility. With a date it sizes a block (clamped 4–16 weeks); with none it runs a rolling 12-week block. Deload every 4th week. maxHR = config.maxHR || 220 − age.

Midlife / menopause overlay

Fires for any real stage (menopauseOverlay() · :298). It composes with the goal — it doesn’t replace it:

  • Strength gains a heavy bone-loading finisher (“heavy finish: 1–2×5 on a main lift”).
  • Hard day becomes SIT sprints (8–10 × 20s) — muscle & metabolism protection in midlife.
  • Recovery nudge appears only if sleep_disruption or low_energy was reported.
  • Skipped on deload weeks.
The method-based engine has a parallel, simpler hook: the menopause strength cue (utils/menopause.ts:100) appends a bone finisher and relabels the day “Strength + bone.”

Findings the harness surfaced

Marathon top pick ≠ Daniels

For an advanced road marathoner the engine selects Fitzgerald 80/20 (score 23), edging Daniels/Hansons (22). Worth knowing — a static reading of the method library would have guessed Daniels.

2:50 off 15 mi/wk: ramp-safe, not goal-checked

The plan peaks at 15 × 2.3 ≈ 34.5 mi/wk and caps pace gain at +8% VDOT. It never validates that a 2:50 is reachable from 15 mi/wk, nor raises volume to chase it. Correct by design — but the goal’s realism is the athlete’s to own.

Taper long-run exceeded the build cap — fixed

The review caught a real bug: the taper branch in weekPlan.ts skipped LONG_MAX_MI, so the 100-miler's first taper week emitted a 40.3 mi long run — longer than any capped build week (34 mi). Fixed: the taper now steps down from the peak build long run (23.7 → 17 → 10.2 mi in P4) and never introduces a new longest run. Locked in by a regression test in generatePlan.test.ts.
